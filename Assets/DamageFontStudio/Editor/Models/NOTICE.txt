DamageTextDetector.onnx is the unmodified ch_PP-OCRv3_det_infer.onnx model.
DamageTextRecognizer.onnx is the unmodified ch_PP-OCRv3_rec_infer.onnx model.
Characters.txt contains the recognizer's original embedded character dictionary.
The surrounding Unity preprocessing, numeric filtering and temporal tracking are
project code. The models locate/read text; they do not recover the source font.
Models stay in the Editor folder and are not required by exported runtime samples.
